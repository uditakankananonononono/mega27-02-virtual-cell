## Candidate with unresolved eligibility: Tong et al. (2020)

Primary article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7527729/ ; Europe PMC full-text XML https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7527729/fullTextXML ; public supplemental archive https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7527729/supplementaryFiles . The paper describes 3,796 Keio E. coli K-12 BW25113 single-gene deletion strains across 30 carbon sources on solid MOPS minimal medium, including glucose. Plates were pinned after a 24-hour nutrient-depletion passage, then scanned every 20 minutes for 24 hours in two technical replicates. The study reports normalized endpoint integrated colony density, not binary survival or liquid growth rate. Supplement Table S1A provides the endpoint matrix and S1B compares a previous MOPS glucose dataset; the archive returned an XLSX file but no gene rows were read. The article says the measurements were compared with EcoCyc v22.0, which is a separate published reconstruction and does not establish independence of our iJO1366/Gerdes-trained stack. CC BY 4.0 is indicated in the open supplement metadata.

Eligibility status: **UNRESOLVED, no score.** The library excludes essential deletions by construction; Keio-derived source material already occurs elsewhere in VC2 and the older Price2018 Keio lineage appears in its data ledger. An untouched *measurement acquisition* is possible but not established by a distinct article or date; check any exact gene/colony measurements and model-development feature sources for reuse before declaring independent. The endpoint (continuous solid-agar colony yield on MOPS + carbon) differs from the Gerdes rich-medium hard-call and from iJO1366 glucose-minimal liquid-equivalent FBA assumptions. Medium and oxygen/model-bound matching must be fixed before using the table, rather than retrofitting an agreement threshold. No eligible new sealed test has yet been registered or performed. The accession/archive itself is available, but does not bridge these dependencies.

## Other leads and exclusions

The Gerdes 2003 labels are the development and original evaluation source, not a new phenotype cohort; the archived Keio-era deletion-call yardstick is already described in `paper/content_b.py` and cannot simply be relabeled untouched. Rousset fitness data use CRISPRi and possible operon polarity, so assay scores are not isolated deletion survival. The 2024 Bleem et al. Pseudomonas KT2440 RB-TnSeq glucose compendium audited in `notes/postresult_2024_rb_tnseq_glucose_screen.md` changes organism and uses competitive transposon fitness; even later acquisition does not make the model-selected 14-gene panel a prospective E. coli benchmark. These exclusions do not claim that the public sources are scientifically poor; they answer this particular gate.

Next screen: establish Tong assay accession/acquisition provenance and overlap against each trained feature, annotation, label and threshold; pin a substrate/oxygen-matched model recipe and count independent gene units without reading candidate outcomes. If this cannot be proven, document the bounded negative and leave G2 FAIL.

## Adjacent 2026 independent-acquisition lead: Champie et al.

Champie et al., *mSystems* (2026), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13185578/ (DOI 10.1128/msystems.01425-25), report a newly generated high-throughput transposon-mutagenesis study in BW25113, with 60 initial mutant populations allocated 20 each to LB, EZ-Rich and MOPS minimal medium plus 0.2% glucose. Five consecutive 24-hour passages led to fiScore gene-impact summaries. Primary publication exposes the gene-level scores in Table S2, source reads at PRJNA1300301, and an insertion-site trackhub. ENA run listing https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/portal/api/search?result=read_run&query=study_accession%3D%22PRJNA1300301%22&fields=run_accession%2Cexperiment_title%2Ccollection_date%2Cfirst_public%2Clibrary_strategy&limit=100&format=tsv confirms Tn-Seq runs, with first-public timestamps in April 2026 for inspected rows (collection dates blank in that endpoint). No gene fiScore rows were inspected.

Eligibility status: **UNRESOLVED, distinct candidate from Tong.** The study compares MOPS gene-impact calls against iML1515 predictions, which supplies useful medium matching but not validation of our supervised stack. Tn-Seq competition/depletion across five passages is not the same endpoint as an isolated deletion colony or Gerdes hard-call viability, and insertion-site/gene polarity must be handled before mapping. It may support a newly registered *fitness-impact* external analysis with fixed model, comparator, insertion-to-gene mapping and threshold; it cannot directly replace the missing condition-matched isolated-knockout gate or retroactively repair Gerdes G2. Sixty initial populations are not 60 independent gene-label datasets. Confirm experimental timing, relation to all training sources and gene coverage before any target outcome use.
