# VC2 new-source eligibility screen, locked 28 September 2026 before any candidate outcome extraction

This source screen follows the failed five-rung Gerdes G2 and the exploratory nested Gerdes follow-up. It cannot rewrite either result. Search public post-development, gene-resolved E. coli K-12 perturbation/growth datasets and record DOI/URL, source release, strain, medium/carbon/oxygen, intervention type, gene identifiers, phenotype measurement, acquisition timing, overlap with Gerdes/Rousset/Keio/model curation, licensing and raw-data access. Include negative and unavailable leads, with a reason per lead. No target-gene outcome, score or threshold is inspected during source eligibility.

A candidate is confirmatory-eligible only if (1) strain/condition and endpoint can be matched in a declared model run, without choosing media after outcomes; (2) gene-resolved raw measurements and a documented independent gene/perturbation unit are public; (3) it was not used to train, select or tune the tested model, its labels, external annotations or threshold, and overlap can be audited; (4) model predictions, feature recipe, inclusion/mapping and threshold are frozen before candidate labels are viewed. Mere publication after model release, a distinct accession or a pooled compendium does not establish (3). Gene knockouts, transposon fitness and CRISPRi are different endpoints; a fitness score is not isolated knockout survival. If no candidate passes all checks, publish the bounded negative source screen and keep G2 failed; do not call a partial match a pass. If one passes, separately preregister a sealed comparison before any outcome extraction, including independent unit, fixed comparator, label mapping, exclusion, uncertainty and pass criterion. Never take a favorable result back to the original five-rung G2.
