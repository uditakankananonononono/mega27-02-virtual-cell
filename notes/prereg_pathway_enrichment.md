# KEGG pathway enrichment of top false-positive predictions

Exploratory analysis fixed before inspection: use frozen v2 OOF scores and Gerdes labels. The query consists of the top 100 v2-scored genes among the experimentally non-essential genes (the hard ranking's most costly false priorities); the reference universe consists of all 1,249 aligned genes. Parse saved KEGG eco gene/pathway links, retain gene sets with at least 5 and at most 500 members in this universe. Use gseapy.enrich (offline custom gene sets) to test one-sided hypergeometric overrepresentation, BH FDR across all tested KEGG pathways. Identify the largest hits by adjusted p, but this is exploratory and there is no pass/fail significance target. Caveat: query selected by model ranking, KEGG covers only subset, and overlapping pathways are dependent. This study validates gseapy as an actually executed analysis tool, not a new independent dataset or an extra KEGG accession.
