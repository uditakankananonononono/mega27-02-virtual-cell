# VC2 judge round 02: post-result biomass-term specificity controls

Written 2026-09-26 after observing 63/68 BiGG model flips and seven Pseudomonas iJN1463 flips. This is an exploratory computational falsification of specificity, not a preregistered independent MoCo win or amendment to Gerdes G2. It was written before the controls were computed but was not committed until after their execution, so its time ordering is not independently established by git; do not treat it as prospective registration. ChatGPT role-play critique asked whether any small biomass deletion would create the same pattern; response is logged verbatim in `results/judge_rounds/round02_response.txt`. The external response has no authority as an actual ISEF adjudicator.

Use the existing hashed iJN1463 snapshot to inspect baseline-essential gene deletion results. The positive intervention removes the three named MoCo-related negative biomass coefficients together. Compare each other single negative objective coefficient with `abs(c)` within a factor of two of any of the three MoCo coefficients; exclude those three MoCo terms themselves. This is a coefficient-matched numerical control, **not** a molecule-class or reaction-connectivity match. The candidate IDs/count were printed locally before the controls ran, but not independently time-stamped; test every candidate, not a hand-picked subset. For each candidate, remove only its own objective coefficient with `subtract_metabolites`; recompute wild type and original baseline-essential knockouts under the same medium and solver, count knockouts restored to >=95% original WT. Save every candidate and count, plus comparison of the three-term MoCo intervention to every single control. The MoCo intervention has three coefficients, so single-term controls are not causally equivalent. A proper multi-term matched test across multiple families needs a separate locked protocol and computational budget.

Interpretation: if many matched-coefficient non-MoCo terms produce comparable or larger rescue, MoCo is an instance of broad biomass sensitivity. If none do, one may say this MoCo *bundle* is more disruptive than these single coefficient-matched terms in one Pseudomonas reconstruction, not that MoCo uniquely controls biology or that 63 models independently replicate in vivo. Preserve failed and positive controls. No new trained predictor, assay outcome, or hard-call gate is scored here.
