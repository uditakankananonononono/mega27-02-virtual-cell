# Candidate KT2440 phenotype sources and validation dependence

27 September 2026, 1:20 PM IST. Research leads only, no new gene-level phenotype scored, no gate update. The 2010 Molina-Henares et al. Pseudomonas putida KT2440 mutant-library paper (PubMed https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20158506/; DOI 10.1111/j.1462-2920.2010.02166.x) screened minimal medium with glucose. Its abstract reports 48 conditionally essential knockout mutants, half among prior model proposals, and 10 available mutants for proposed model-essential genes that proved nonessential under the tested condition. Those are paper-level findings, not our model's gene-wise validation. Exact nutrient formulation, IDs, knockout coverage, phenotype calls and supplemental files have not been retrieved and matched to our iJN1463 model.

The 2019 Nogales et al. reconstruction paper (https://www.osti.gov/pages/biblio/1574030; DOI 10.1111/1462-2920.14843) reports gene-essentiality predictions compared with a knockout library and Bar-seq data, and lists the 2010 paper among references. Its abstract calls the reconstruction iJN1462; the BiGG snapshot we use is named iJN1463. Do not equate the two without source-version mapping. This published comparison is a dependency warning: the 2010 phenotypes might have influenced reconstruction, curation or model evaluation, so they are not automatically an untouched independent validation cohort.

The 2024 Bleem et al. RB-TnSeq meta-analysis (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10949508/; DOI 10.1128/msystems.00942-23) aggregates 332 samples from 179 conditions and 4,732 measured genes, including growth on carbon and nitrogen sources. The paper warns of transposon polarity in operons. Its contributing assay overlap with the 2019 reconstruction validation remains untraced. The June 2026 Fitness Browser archive (https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Archive_of_the_RB-TnSeq_Fitness_Browser_June_2026/32680503) advertises a compressed SQLite database and organism-specific fitness files; we have not retrieved exact KT2440 glucose-condition rows or checked accession-level overlap.

Next action is source archaeology, not scoring: obtain the original source supplements and experiment metadata, establish model-version lineage and whether each assay informed reconstruction, identify source gene IDs and condition-matched glucose medium, then commit a validation protocol before opening outcome rows against the saved rescued-gene list. If independence cannot be established, use these data only as a labeled reused-evidence contradiction check. Preserve original failed G2 and current no-phenotype-validation caveat.
