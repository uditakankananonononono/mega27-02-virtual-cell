The -1 marker was fixed only as a descriptive benchmark, not a growth/survival or validated essentiality cutoff. Full per-gene pairs and source checks are in `results/postresult_rb_tnseq_crossset_repeatability.json`; rerun `scripts/audit_postresult_rb_tnseq_crossset_repeatability.py`.

Both sets remain reused, selected-after-model source assays, with possible transposon polarity and unknown relation to the 2019 reconstruction validation. The omitted three genes have no workbook rows. Nothing measures clean isolated knockout viability or edited-biomass cellular rescue; the original five-rung G2 remains FAIL. This mixed within-compendium stability merits a small caveat in the paper, not a new headline.
