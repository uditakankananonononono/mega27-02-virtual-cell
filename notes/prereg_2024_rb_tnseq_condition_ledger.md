Published 2019 reconstruction used earlier BarSeq glucose/acetate/p-coumarate and PRCC data; later assays might be useful only after source-identity, date, media, and publication-overlap checks.

Freeze a source-screen before outcomes: enumerate every sample whose metadata experiment description and medium explicitly include glucose as sole added carbon and no other stressor or aromatic compound; record set, run date, library, person, replicate and exact medium, as well as workbook column mapping. Require provenance after the published reconstruction and no overlap with its S7 assay identifiers; if metadata insufficient, flag independence UNKNOWN. Do not choose one glucose concentration or replicate after looking at target fitness. Match all 14 fixed target IDs, preserve missing values. For any truly distinct post-reconstruction candidate, define a descriptive no-fit rule: each target's replicate median fitness < -2.7 is a strong depletion indicator only, NOT a prevalidated phenotype threshold across different assay protocols; use raw values and signs first, and separately compare with authors' own significance/T-like conventions if documented. Never call a model's relative fitness a direct viability test. Do not promote a gate based on a source whose lineage or medium remains uncertain. First commit this protocol, then extract metadata; decide whether to inspect outcomes once provenance is resolved. G2 remains FAIL.
