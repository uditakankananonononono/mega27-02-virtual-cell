# VC2 executive summary, 27 September 2026

**Question.** Which gene-essentiality predictions of a virtual-cell model reflect its biomass-objective choice rather than a robust network limitation, and which predictor comparisons remain valid after checking fold and threshold provenance?

**What is new here.** A runnable audit varies model biomass demands, traces gene-call changes, checks them with independent linear-program and gene-rule implementations, and separates historical stacked rankings from score-lineage-safe comparisons. This is a model-sensitivity and evaluation-method contribution. It is not evidence that a predicted knockout survives in a living organism.

**Key model result.** In the saved BiGG survey, 63/68 scorable MoCo-positive snapshots changed at least one predicted gene call after MoCo-coefficient removal. Most are related reconstructions, so 63/68 is not 63 independent experiments. In a separately checked Pseudomonas model, three-term removal rescued seven zero-growth knockout predictions. Across its 102 individually tested biomass terms, 23 single removals rescued at least one call, but no individual MoCo term did; pairwise controls also challenged MoCo uniqueness. Sources: results/bigg_moco_growth_summary.json, results/bigg_second_family_verification.json, and Section 7's original single/pair audits.

**Evaluation result.** The locked Gerdes hard-call G2 gate failed at every one of five rungs. The threshold lineage audit found upstream label reuse, so the hard-call comparison is descriptive. The historical stacked ranking increase of +0.057 AUROC over FBA is not the same contrast as the later +0.024 AUROC of a leakage-controlled v2 feature set over a rescaled v1 model. Rich-medium Gerdes labels and glucose-minimal FBA are not a condition-matched phenotype experiment. Sources: results/calibration_gate.json, results/calibration_lineage_audit.json, results/ensemble_v2.json.

**What remains.** A fully nested evaluation or untouched phenotype cohort; condition-matched knockout assays and mechanistic perturbations; reproducible tests across model families; Rousset operon-polarity sensitivity. The paper's 50+ substantive text-body-page requirement is not met by its current total PDF page count, because appendices and references carry much of it. Negative analyses remain in the repository and backup presentation, not discarded.
