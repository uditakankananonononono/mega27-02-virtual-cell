## Previously published Keio LB/M63 phenotype overlap (post-packet novelty audit, 14:25 IST)

The 2020 primary article by Liu et al., *Scientific Reports* https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-72389-6 , explicitly assessed growth rates of 3,909 Keio BW25113 knockout strains in LB and M63, linked rates to wild-type expression, and reported 115 differentially growing genes under medium change at FDR q < 0.05. The 2026 Lao/Ying article itself cites it: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-07075-9 . The 2024 Aida/Ying paper also evaluated genetic/environmental growth variation in a 114-strain/134-medium design: https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-07347-3 . Therefore observing LB-versus-M63 Keio growth, or ranking responsive genes by that contrast, is prior art rather than a new discovery. Do not claim the 2020 and 2026 raw acquisition files are identical: that has **not** been verified. Their shared genotype universe and assay question are known; independence of measurement acquisitions remains open. A future novelty test would need a clearly distinct, ancestry-clean mechanistic prediction beyond these analyses, with a separate frozen external evaluation, not a relabeling of the same contrasts.

## Assay-to-model go/no-go matrix

1. **Strain/perturbation: open.** Target is Keio BW25113 deletion stocks; model reference and Gerdes labels are MG1655-context. Demonstrate exact gene/allele mapping, remove unambiguous nonorthologs by a rule fixed before reading target outcomes, and do not assign zero growth to genes absent from the viable collection.
2. **Medium/flux: open.** M63 recipe from Methods: 62 mM K2HPO4, 39 mM KH2PO4, 15 mM (NH4)2SO4, 1.8 μM FeSO4, 15 μM thiamine-HCl, 0.2 mM MgSO4, 22 mM glucose. The local `vcell/metabolism.py` `Condition` panel uses one carbon exchange, a default -10 uptake bound and O2 open at -1000 (or closed). Those are modeling assumptions, not calibrated by ingredient concentration or 567-cpm plate shaking. A fixed exchange/bounds recipe and WT check are needed before outcome reading; if oxygen/maintenance cannot be identified, name a bounded sensitivity analysis, not a unique condition match.
3. **Stock selection/quality: open.** The article reports 46 strains whose non-shaking overnight stock wells had no visible growth and were instead cultured in shaken tubes until growth, recorded in v2 stock metadata. Predeclare retain/exclude/stratify. The same well position persists across stock and assay plates. OD600 scattering can change with morphology; K/r have incomplete-phase, noisy-slope and replicate-outlier flags. Fix quality exclusions and one primary endpoint before opening values.
4. **Score/comparator: open.** The supervised v2 `v2_lr` values are OOF on Gerdes genes, not an externally frozen prediction for all 3,909 Keio strains. A single train-only fit/feature freeze and an M63-matched FBA comparator on the same eligible gene set must be specified independently of candidate outcomes. Do not threshold Keio curves by optimizing on them.
5. **Uncertainty/pass: open.** A new dated protocol must name the strain-level estimand (for example a continuous rank contrast of growth under M63), replicate aggregation, plate/stock sensitivity, fixed comparator, confidence calculation, multiplicity and exact pass criterion. No result can revise the original Gerdes G2.

**Unblock condition:** independently audit feature/label provenance and target overlap, define a defensible versioned M63 model/endpoint/comparator and exclusions, then lock a *separate* test before opening Figshare outcome rows. If any cannot be established, this remains a bounded negative eligibility finding, not a scored experiment. No heavy computation is launched by this packet.
